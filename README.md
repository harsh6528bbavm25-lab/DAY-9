# DAY 9 — LAB 9
## Marketing Copy and Compliance

### 📌 Overview
This lab focuses on creating professional and compliant marketing content for the aviation industry. It demonstrates how a single marketing brief can be adapted for different platforms while keeping all verified facts, conditions, and claims accurate.

### ✈️ Topics Covered
- Aviation marketing communication
- Platform-specific promotional content
- LinkedIn and Instagram marketing
- Advertisement headline creation
- Promotional email writing
- Marketing compliance and fact-checking
- AI-generated content review
- Identifying misleading or unsupported claims
- Fact-difference and register checks
- Peer review and human approval

### 🎯 Campaigns Covered

#### 1. AeroVista Student Saver
A student-fare campaign offering eligible college and university students **up to 10% off the base fare** on selected domestic routes. The campaign includes specific booking and travel periods, student-ID requirements, limited availability, and applicable fare rules. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="1"}

#### 2. AeroConnect Airport Career Fair 2026
A career-focused aviation campaign designed to help college and university students explore aviation careers, attend information sessions, interact with industry representatives, and receive career guidance. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

### 🛡️ Compliance Focus
The lab emphasizes avoiding unsupported claims such as guaranteed discounts, free baggage, guaranteed employment, guaranteed internships, or claims of being the cheapest or best. All promotional content must remain consistent with verified campaign information. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="3"}

### 🤖 AI Usage
AI tools such as **ChatGPT and Google Gemini** were used to generate initial marketing drafts. The content was then independently reviewed for factual accuracy, audience suitability, brand consistency, and compliance. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="4"}

### 📚 Learning Outcomes
After completing this lab, I learned:
- How to adapt marketing content for different platforms.
- How to distinguish facts from promotional claims.
- How to identify misleading AI-generated content.
- How to perform fact-difference and register checks.
- Why important offer conditions must be clearly communicated.
- Why human approval is necessary before publishing AI-generated marketing content.
